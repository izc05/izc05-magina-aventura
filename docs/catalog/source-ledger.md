## Current official identity baseline

The canonical draft set contains 17 official trail identities: Adelfal de Cuadros, Caño del Aguadero, Castillo de Albanchez, Castillo de Mata Bejid, El Peralejo, Fuenmayor, Gibralberca, Hoyalinos, La Cueva de la Graja, Las Viñas, Pinar de Cánava, Puerto de la Mata, Sierra de la Cruz, Subida al Hoyo de la Laguna, Subida a Pico Mágina y Miramundos, Umbría de los Corzos, and Veredón-Mojón Blanco.

Ten currently have route-level technical sheets recorded in the machine catalog: Adelfal de Cuadros, Castillo de Albanchez, Castillo de Mata Bejid, Gibralberca, Hoyalinos, La Cueva de la Graja, Las Viñas, Subida al Hoyo de la Laguna, Subida a Pico Mágina y Miramundos, and Veredón-Mojón Blanco. The remaining seven keep unknown technical fields rather than receiving inferred values.

All 17 remain `draft` until route-level geometry is fetched and validated where required. No community track is promoted as official geometry.

## Geometry note

The Las Viñas official page exposes KML/GML downloads from the REDIAM WFS (`senderos:senderos`, `CODIGOEQUI=724`, source CRS shown as EPSG:25830). Veredón-Mojón Blanco also exposes downloadable route geometry on its official page. V1 records these as geometry-source leads, but does not commit a `TrackAsset` until the original file can be fetched, transformed when needed, and validated reproducibly.

The current execution environment cannot resolve/fetch the REDIAM WFS endpoint reliably, so geometry remains intentionally pending rather than being substituted by community tracks.

## Operational-status rule

The absence of a closure record is not evidence that a route is open. `open` is reserved for explicit current authoritative confirmation. Adelfal de Cuadros and Las Viñas therefore derive `closed` while their current official pages remain marked temporarily closed/out of service and the Cuadros notice remains active.
